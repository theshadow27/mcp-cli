## What worked

- **Three-lane serial structure.** The sprint-78 stacking failure did not recur.
- **Worker judgement, consistently.** Sessions caught orchestrator errors before
  they cost anything, correctly classified contention artefacts without
  repairing healthy diffs, refused to grade their own work, and declined to
  present failed-run data as a passing run's.
- **The asymmetric artefact rule.** *A pass under load is a strong pass; a
  timeout-shaped failure under load proves nothing — capture, mail, do not
  repair or retry.* Prevented at least three healthy diffs from being "fixed".
- **Bun 1.4.0 validation.** Four independent runs, 7028 pass / 0 fail, and
  `Spec floor: 247 discovered / 247 expected` — `checkDiscoveryFloor` (#2719)
  armed and satisfied, proving the upgrade moved spec discovery by zero files.
  CI then passed 5/5 on the new pin, self-proving.
